Why choose WordPress for your business website

WordPress suits businesses that publish often, need lots of service and location pages, or want full control of their site and hosting. You own the files, you can move hosts whenever you like, and almost any feature you need has a reliable, well-supported option.

  • Easy editing. Change text, images and pages in a visual editor, without touching code.
  • Built for content. Service pages, area pages and blog posts are simple to add as you grow.
  • SEO control. Full control over title tags, headings, URLs, schema and redirects.
  • No platform lock-in. Your site, your hosting and your data, all in your name.

How we keep WordPress sites fast and secure

Speed problems on WordPress usually come from heavy page builders, oversized images and plugins that load scripts on every page. We use a lean theme and the block editor, compress and lazy-load images, and only load scripts where they're used.

For security, we set up managed hosting with daily backups, automatic minor updates, a firewall and limited admin accounts. Your team gets an editor login with the access it needs, and we keep the administrator keys safe.

What's included in a WordPress website

  • Custom design. A unique design built around your brand and customers, not a bought theme.
  • Reusable blocks. Pre-styled blocks for services, FAQs, testimonials and calls to action, so new pages stay on brand.
  • Local SEO setup. Title tags, H1s, NAP in the footer, LocalBusiness schema and an XML sitemap.
  • Contact forms. Forms that send to your inbox and a Google Sheet, with spam protection.
  • Training. A recorded walkthrough showing your team how to edit pages and publish posts.

Is WordPress Website Design right for your business?

WordPress is a strong choice for most service businesses, but not every business needs it. It suits you best if these sound familiar.

  • You'll publish regularly. Blog posts, case studies, new service pages or location pages added every month.
  • You want full ownership. Your own hosting, your own files and the freedom to change developers at any time.
  • You need flexibility. Bookings, quote forms, galleries or integrations that may change as you grow.

What affects the cost of WordPress Website Design

WordPress projects are quoted at a fixed price. The main things that affect it are below, and ongoing hosting is billed separately in your name.

  • Design scope. How many unique page layouts are needed, from a simple brochure site to a full set of templates.
  • Custom blocks. Reusable sections for services, testimonials, pricing tables or team profiles.
  • Plugins and integrations. Booking, CRM, email marketing or payment connections.
  • Content migration. Moving posts and pages from an old site, and setting up redirects.

WordPress Website Design FAQs

Ask anything else in the form below and we'll cover it on our call.

Is WordPress good for SEO?

Yes, when it's set up properly. WordPress gives you full control over titles, headings, URLs, schema and redirects. Most WordPress SEO problems come from slow themes and duplicate pages, which we avoid in the build.

Will I be able to edit the site myself?

Yes. You'll edit pages in the WordPress block editor using blocks we've styled for your brand, and you'll get a recorded walkthrough to refer back to.

Do you use page builders like Elementor or Divi?

We avoid heavy page builders because they slow sites down. We use the native block editor with custom blocks, which keeps editing simple and pages fast.

Who hosts the website?

We recommend managed WordPress hosting in your name, with backups and security included. We can set it up for you or work with your current host.

Can you move my existing site to WordPress?

Yes. We migrate your content, keep URLs where possible and set up 301 redirects for anything that changes, so you keep your search rankings.

Do you offer website maintenance after launch?

Launch support is included for 30 days. After that, we can keep plugins, themes and backups up to date on a monthly plan, or hand everything to your team.
