Why we usually recommend Shopify

Shopify handles hosting, security, payments and checkout, so your team can focus on products and customers. It supports Australian and New Zealand payment methods, buy-now-pay-later options, GST settings and local shipping carriers through its app ecosystem. For most small and mid-sized retailers it's the fastest way to a reliable store.

If your business has unusual requirements, such as complex B2B pricing or bookings mixed with products, we'll talk through WooCommerce or a custom setup before you commit.

Ecommerce SEO built into the store

Product and collection pages are your best chance to rank for buying searches. We write unique collection descriptions, set clean URLs, add Product and Breadcrumb schema, and make sure filters don't create thousands of duplicate pages for Google to crawl.

  • Collection pages. Category pages that target searches like 'linen dresses NZ' or 'work boots Brisbane'.
  • Product schema. Price, availability and reviews marked up so search results can show rich details.
  • Image optimisation. Compressed images with descriptive file names and alt text.
  • Internal links. Related products and collections linked so customers and Google can find more.

What's included in an ecommerce website

  • Store design. Homepage, collection, product, cart and content pages designed for your brand.
  • Catalogue setup. Products, variants, collections and inventory imported and organised.
  • Payments and shipping. Payment gateways, GST, shipping zones and rates configured.
  • Tracking. GA4 ecommerce events, Meta pixel and Google Ads conversion tags.
  • Email capture. Pop-up or footer sign-up connected to your email platform for abandoned-cart and welcome flows.

Is Shopify & Ecommerce Websites right for your business?

Shopify is the best fit for most small and medium retailers in Australia and New Zealand. It's especially well suited to these businesses.

  • Product brands selling online. Businesses with a catalogue of physical products shipped nationally.
  • Shops with a physical location. Retailers who want online sales and in-store pickup from the same system.
  • Brands planning to grow. Stores that will add products, run promotions and invest in email and ads.

What affects the cost of Shopify & Ecommerce Websites

Store builds are quoted at a fixed price. Shopify's monthly plan and any paid apps are billed directly to you.

  • Catalogue size. The number of products, variants and collections to set up or import.
  • Design. A refined theme versus fully custom templates for product and collection pages.
  • Apps and integrations. Reviews, subscriptions, wholesale pricing, shipping and accounting connections.
  • Migration. Moving products, customers and orders from another platform, with redirects.

Shopify & Ecommerce Websites FAQs

Ask anything else in the form below and we'll cover it on our call.

How much does a Shopify store cost to build?

It depends on the number of products, custom features and integrations. You'll get a fixed quote after the discovery call, and Shopify's own monthly plan is billed directly to you.

Can you move my store from WooCommerce or another platform?

Yes. We migrate products, customers and order history where the platforms allow, and redirect old product URLs so you keep your search rankings.

Do you set up GST and shipping?

Yes. We configure tax settings and shipping zones for Australia, New Zealand or international orders, and connect carrier apps if you need live rates.

Will my store rank on Google?

Your store will be built with ecommerce SEO foundations. Ranking for competitive product searches usually also needs ongoing content and link building, which we can add as an SEO plan.

Can I manage products myself after launch?

Yes. Shopify's admin is easy to learn, and we include a walkthrough on adding products, running discounts and managing orders.

Can my store sell to both Australia and New Zealand?

Yes. Shopify supports multiple markets, so we can set up prices, currencies, tax settings and shipping for both countries.
