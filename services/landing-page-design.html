What makes a landing page convert

A landing page has one job. It should feel like the next step after the ad, explain the offer in seconds and make the form or phone number impossible to miss. Most underperforming pages fail on one of these basics.

  • Message match. The headline repeats the promise from the ad, so visitors know they're in the right place.
  • A clear offer. A free audit, quote, consultation or guide that's worth the visitor's details.
  • A short form. Only the fields you need, placed where people can see it without scrolling far.
  • Proof and detail. What's included, how it works, FAQs and your real business details.
  • Speed. Pages that load in under two and a half seconds on a mid-range phone.

Landing pages and lead magnets

Lead magnets work best when each one has its own page. A free homepage concept, a 90-day growth plan or a downloadable checklist each attracts a different visitor, so each gets its own headline, form and follow-up. We connect every page to your lead sheet with the ad source attached, so you can see which offer and which ad brought in each lead.

We also set up the thank-you page as a conversion event in Google Ads and GA4. That gives the ad platforms clean data to optimise against, which usually lowers cost per lead over the following weeks.

What's included

  • Page design and copy. Headline options, offer, sections and form, written for your audience.
  • Form connected to your systems. Leads sent to a Google Sheet or CRM with UTM and click ID data.
  • Conversion tracking. Google Ads, GA4 and Meta conversion events on the thank-you page.
  • A/B test plan. The first two or three tests to run, ranked by likely impact.

Is Landing Page Design right for your business?

A dedicated landing page is worth building whenever you're paying for traffic or promoting a specific offer. It's most valuable in these cases.

  • You run Google or Meta ads. Every campaign should send clicks to a page that matches its promise.
  • You have a lead magnet. A free audit, quote, guide or consultation that deserves its own page and form.
  • Your homepage isn't converting. A focused page lets you test offers without redesigning the whole site.

What affects the cost of Landing Page Design

Landing pages are priced per page, with a lower rate for variations of the same page. These factors affect the price.

  • Copywriting. Whether we write the page from scratch or refine copy you already have.
  • Design. Using your site's existing components or designing new sections.
  • Integrations. Connecting forms to a Google Sheet, CRM or email platform with full tracking.
  • Testing. Building extra versions for A/B tests on headlines, offers or layouts.

Landing Page Design FAQs

Ask anything else in the form below and we'll cover it on our call.

Why not send ad traffic to my homepage?

Your homepage talks to everyone. A landing page talks to the person who clicked one specific ad, with one offer and one action, which usually converts at a much higher rate.

How many landing pages do I need?

One per offer or service you advertise. If you run ads for three services, three focused pages will usually outperform one general page.

Can you build landing pages on my existing website?

Yes. We can build them on your current platform, or as standalone pages on a subdomain if your site is slow or hard to edit.

Do you write the copy?

Yes. We write the headline, offer and page copy, and you approve everything before it goes live.

How do you measure success?

Conversion rate and cost per lead from your ad platform, checked against the leads that actually arrive in your sheet or CRM.

How fast can a landing page go live?

Most landing pages are live within one to two weeks of the brief, including copy, design, tracking and testing.
