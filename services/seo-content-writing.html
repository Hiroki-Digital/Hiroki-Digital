Service and location pages that show Google what you do and where you do it.

Hiroki Digital writes service pages, location pages and supporting articles for businesses across Australia and New Zealand. Every page is planned around a real search, written for people first and linked into the rest of your site.

Building topical authority

Topical authority means covering your field thoroughly. A skincare clinic that has a page for each treatment, such as microneedling, chemical peels and acne treatment, looks more expert to Google than one with a single 'treatments' page.

We structure content in a clear hierarchy: your homepage, a services page, then a page for each core service. Each service page answers what it is, who it's for, how it works, what it costs to consider and common questions, in 1,000 to 2,000 words of useful copy.

Building local authority

Local authority proves you work in the areas you say you do. We create an 'Areas we serve' page and a page for each key suburb, town or region, written with real local detail and without pretending you have an office you don't.

  • Hyperlocal detail. Suburbs, landmarks, local conditions and the questions people in that area ask.
  • Services offered there. Links to the services most relevant to each area.
  • Directions and contact. How to reach you, your service radius and a clear call to action.
  • Internal links. Service pages link to area pages and back, with natural, varied anchor text.

How we write

We start with research: what your customers search, what the top-ranking pages cover and what questions they leave unanswered. We use AI tools to speed up drafting, but every page is planned, edited and fact-checked by a person, using details from your business. Pages include FAQs, schema recommendations and suggested images with alt text.

Is SEO Content Writing right for your business?

Content is the advanced pass of local SEO. It's the right step when the basics are in place and you need more pages for Google to rank.

  • Your site has fewer than 30 pages. Not enough pages for Google to understand all your services and areas.
  • You serve several areas. Each suburb, town or region deserves a page that proves you work there.
  • You're stuck outside the top three. Profile and on-page fixes are done, but rankings have stalled.

What affects the cost of SEO Content Writing

Content is priced per page or as a monthly package. These factors affect the price of each page.

  • Page length and depth. Short supporting pages versus 1,500 to 2,500-word pillar pages.
  • Research required. Technical or regulated topics need more research and fact-checking.
  • Your input. Interviews and examples from your business make content stronger and more specific.
  • Publishing. Whether we write only, or also format, add images, link and publish each page.

SEO Content Writing FAQs

Ask anything else in the form below and we'll cover it on our call.

How long should a service page be?

Long enough to answer the question fully. For most service pages that's 1,000 to 2,000 words, including FAQs. Thin pages rarely rank.

Do you use AI to write content?

We use AI to speed up research and first drafts. Every page is planned, edited and checked by a person, and includes real details from your business.

Will location pages get me penalised?

Not when they're genuinely useful. Pages copied with only the suburb name changed can be treated as doorway pages. Ours are written for each area with local detail.

Do I need to provide information?

A short interview and your service details are enough. You review each page before it's published.

How many pages will I need?

Most local businesses need at least 30 indexed pages to compete, made up of service pages, area pages and helpful articles.

Can you write blog posts as well as service pages?

Yes. Once your service and area pages are in place, supporting articles help build topical authority and answer common questions.
