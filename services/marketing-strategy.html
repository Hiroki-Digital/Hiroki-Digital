What a marketing strategy covers

We keep strategy practical. Every section ends with actions, owners and dates.

  • Customers. Who buys from you, why they choose you and what they search for.
  • Positioning. How you're different from competitors, in plain language customers understand.
  • Offers. Lead magnets and entry offers that make it easy for people to take the first step.
  • Channels. Which mix of Google SEO, Google Ads, social, email and referrals suits your business.
  • Budget. How much to spend on each channel, and what result to expect for it.
  • Measurement. The numbers to track each month, and the targets that matter.

Brand growth for small businesses

Brand growth doesn't need a big budget. It comes from showing up consistently in the places your customers look: a complete Google Business Profile, a steady flow of reviews, a website that matches your ads, and useful posts and photos every week. Consistency compounds, and customers start to recognise you before they need you.

We include a simple weekly routine in every strategy: one Google Business Profile post, new photos of real work and a review request process. It takes less than an hour a week and builds both brand recognition and local rankings.

Is Marketing Strategy right for your business?

A written strategy helps most when you're about to spend money on marketing, or when you already are and can't tell what's working. Common situations include these.

  • You're launching or relaunching. A new business, service, location or brand.
  • You're spending without a plan. Ads, social posts and agencies running in parallel with no shared goal.
  • You've hit a plateau. Enquiries have levelled off and you need to find the next growth channel.

What affects the cost of Marketing Strategy

Strategy work is quoted as a fixed project. The scope depends on the factors below.

  • Business complexity. One service in one area, or several services across several regions.
  • Research depth. Competitor analysis, keyword research, customer interviews and channel costs.
  • Number of channels. How many channels the plan covers in detail.
  • Follow-up. Whether you'd like check-ins at 30, 60 and 90 days to adjust the plan.

Marketing Strategy for businesses across Australia & New Zealand

Strategy changes from market to market. Competition and costs in Melbourne look very different from Hobart, so every plan starts with research in your own area. Many plans start with local SEO because it builds an asset you keep.

Marketing Strategy FAQs

Ask anything else in the form below and we'll cover it on our call.

Is the free 90-day growth plan the same as a full strategy?

The free growth plan is a shorter version focused on your quickest wins. A full strategy goes deeper into positioning, offers, budgets and measurement.

Do I have to hire you to carry out the strategy?

No. The plan is yours to use with your own team or another provider. If you'd like, we can also handle part of it for you.

How long does it take?

Most strategies are delivered within two to three weeks of the discovery workshop.

What if I have a very small budget?

Then the plan focuses on low-cost channels such as Google Business Profile, reviews, local SEO and email, which build results over time.

Do you work with startups?

Yes. For new businesses, the plan focuses on validating the offer and finding the first channel that brings customers in profitably.

What do I need to prepare for the workshop?

Just a rough idea of your numbers: what a customer is worth, where enquiries come from today and what you currently spend on marketing.

Can the strategy include SEO and a new website?

Yes. The plan covers whichever channels make sense, including SEO, your website, ads, email and referrals.
