Digital Marketing FAQs

Ask anything else in the form and we'll cover it on our call.

What budget do I need?

Your growth plan includes a recommended starting budget based on your market, competition and goals. Many local businesses start with a modest test budget and scale up once cost per lead is proven.

Which platforms do you manage?

Google Ads, Meta (Facebook and Instagram), LinkedIn for B2B, and email platforms such as Mailchimp and Klaviyo.

How quickly will I see results?

Search ads can bring enquiries in the first week or two. Expect 4 to 8 weeks of testing before campaigns are fully optimised and cost per lead settles.

Do you lock me into a contract?

No. All marketing plans are month-to-month. You can pause or cancel with 30 days' notice.

Do you create the ads?

Yes. We write the copy, design the graphics and edit short videos. If you have brand assets or photos, we'll use them.

How do you report on results?

You get a live dashboard, a one-page monthly report and a 30-minute review call. Every report leads with leads, cost per lead and revenue where we can track it.
