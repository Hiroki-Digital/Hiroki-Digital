hirokidigital.com›faq

SEO & Website Design FAQs

Can't see yours? Ask it in the form below and we'll answer it on our call.

Do I need all three services?

No. Many businesses start with one, usually a new website or SEO, and add the others later. The free audit tells you which one will make the biggest difference first.

How much does it cost?

Websites are quoted at a fixed price after the free audit, based on the pages and features you need. SEO and marketing are monthly retainers with no lock-in contract. You'll always see the price before any work starts.

How long until I see results?

Most websites launch in 3 to 6 weeks. Search ads can bring enquiries within the first week or two. SEO is slower to build, usually 3 to 6 months for meaningful traffic growth, but it keeps working without a cost per click.

Will I own my website and accounts?

Yes. Your domain, website, Google Ads, Analytics and Business Profile accounts are set up in your name. If you ever move on, everything goes with you.

Who will I be working with?

A small team, with one main contact from your first call to your monthly reports. No account managers passing messages along.

Which areas do you work in?

We work with businesses across Australia and New Zealand. Everything runs over video calls, email and shared reports, so location is never a problem.
