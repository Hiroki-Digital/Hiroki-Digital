Search along a long, narrow city

The Gold Coast runs in a long strip, from Coomera and Helensvale in the north to Coolangatta at the border. Searchers in Burleigh see different map results from those in Southport, so a business serving the whole coast needs a plan for each stretch.

Demand also moves with the seasons. School holidays, summer and major events bring visitors who search for food, activities and services they need quickly. We plan posts, offers and ad budgets around those peaks so you're visible when demand is highest.

How we approach SEO for Gold Coast businesses

We run heatmaps along the coast to show where you rank from north to south. Then we compare the top businesses on reviews, photos, categories and services. On the Gold Coast, photos matter a lot; visitors judge a venue or tour by what they can see.

On the website, we add your service and Gold Coast to title tags and headings, put your details in the footer, add schema and a map, and build pages for each part of the coast you serve. Weekly posts and geotagged photos keep your profile active through the seasons.

Tourism and hospitality businesses get the most from a complete Google Business Profile with fresh photos. Service businesses usually add local SEO by area, and retailers often pair it with an online store.

Local SEO checklist for Gold Coast businesses

Whether you work with us or not, these are the steps that make the biggest difference to local rankings in Gold Coast.

  1. Keep seasonal hours up to date. Update special hours for school holidays and public holidays so visitors aren't turned away.
  2. Upload fresh photos often. Visitors choose venues and tours by what they can see. Recent, real photos make a difference.
  3. Target both locals and visitors. Write for 'near me' searches as well as suburb and service searches.
  4. Build pages along the coast. If you serve the whole coast, create pages for the north, central and southern areas.
  5. Answer common questions. Add FAQs to your profile and website about parking, bookings, accessibility and pricing.
  6. Track bookings properly. Make sure every booking or enquiry is tracked so you know which channel drove it.

SEO & web design in Gold Coast: FAQs

Ask anything else in the form below and we'll cover it on our call.

Can you help me rank for both locals and tourists?

Yes. Locals and visitors search differently, so we target both: service and suburb searches for locals, and 'near me' and activity searches for visitors.

I serve the whole coast. Can I rank everywhere?

Your profile ranks strongest near your location. Area pages, reviews from customers along the coast and Google Ads help cover the rest.

Do you plan around school holidays and peak season?

Yes. We schedule posts, offers and ad budgets around peak periods when search demand is highest.

Do you work with businesses in the Tweed or Brisbane as well?

Yes. If you serve those areas, we build pages and track rankings there too.

How important are photos for my profile?

Very. Businesses with more recent, real photos tend to attract more clicks and calls, and photos help Google understand what you offer.

Can you build a booking-friendly website?

Yes. We design fast, mobile-first sites with clear booking buttons and tracking, so you can see where bookings come from.

Can you help a tour or activity business?

Yes. For tours and activities, your Google Business Profile, photos, reviews and a fast booking page do most of the work.

Should I advertise during peak season or off-peak?

Both, differently. Peak season is about being visible when demand is high; off-peak is the time to build content and reviews.

Can you help a Gold Coast business that also serves Northern NSW?

Yes. We build pages for the Tweed and other areas you serve, and include them in your rankings tracking.
