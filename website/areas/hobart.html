Local SEO checklist for Hobart businesses

Whether you work with us or not, these are the steps that make the biggest difference to local rankings in Hobart.

  1. Set accurate seasonal hours. Opening hours change through the year for many Hobart businesses. Keep Google up to date.
  2. Show off your location. Photos of your premises, the waterfront or your products help visitors decide.
  3. Cover both sides of the river. If you serve the eastern shore and Kingston, say so and build pages for them.
  4. List every service and product. A full services list helps you appear for the specific searches visitors make.
  5. Make your site fast on mobile. Visitors search on their phones, often on patchy connections.
  6. Collect reviews from locals and visitors. A mix of reviews shows you're trusted by both.
