How we approach SEO for Wellington businesses

We run a heatmap across your service area and compare the top 10 for your keyword on reviews, photos, categories and services. Then we fix your profile: categories, services and descriptions, profile features, FAQs and weekly activity.

On the website, we add your service and Wellington to title tags and headings, your details to the footer, schema and a map, and pages for each area you serve. Citations on New Zealand platforms such as Yellow and Finda follow, along with the directories that rank for your keyword.

Wellington businesses often start with a complete Google Business Profile and clear service content. If your site feels slow on mobile, speed optimisation is a quick improvement.

Local SEO checklist for Wellington businesses

Whether you work with us or not, these are the steps that make the biggest difference to local rankings in Wellington.

  1. Cover the whole region you serve. If you work in the Hutt Valley, Porirua or the Kāpiti Coast, build pages for them.
  2. Keep your CBD details exact. In a dense CBD, accurate addresses, entrances and hours matter to customers on foot.
  3. Show your expertise. For professional and public-sector buyers, clear service pages and credentials build trust.
  4. List every service. A full services list on your profile helps you appear for specific searches.
  5. Use NZ citations. Yellow, Finda and industry directories support your local rankings.
  6. Ask for reviews after every job. A steady stream of reviews keeps you ahead of nearby competitors.
