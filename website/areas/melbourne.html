What makes Melbourne different

Melbourne's inner suburbs are dense. In places like Fitzroy, Richmond or South Yarra, dozens of similar businesses sit within walking distance of each other, so small differences in reviews, photos and categories decide who appears in the map pack.

Further out, the city spreads across a very large area. A business in Dandenong or Werribee may serve customers spread over many kilometres. Here, area pages, citations and a well-defined service area on your Google Business Profile matter more, because proximity alone won't cover the whole region.

How we approach SEO for Melbourne businesses

We start with a heatmap and a look at the top 10 businesses for your main keyword. In busy Melbourne categories, the leaders often have far more photos and reviews than everyone else, and the benchmark shows exactly how big that gap is for you. We also check whether the businesses ranking above you are real; keyword-stuffed listings do turn up and can be reported.

Then we work through the profile, the website and citations. For inner-city businesses, weekly photos and posts make a visible difference because competitors are so close. For businesses serving a wide area, we build pages for each region and link them to your services.

For dense inner-city areas, a fully optimised Google Business Profile is often the quickest win. Businesses serving wider areas benefit from service and suburb pages and a website that's easy to grow.

Local SEO checklist for Melbourne businesses

Whether you work with us or not, these are the steps that make the biggest difference to local rankings in Melbourne.

  1. Keep hours and menus accurate. For hospitality, wrong opening hours on Google cost customers immediately, especially around public holidays.
  2. Use your neighbourhood names. Melburnians search with suburb names like Fitzroy, Richmond or Box Hill. Use them naturally on your site.
  3. Fill in every service. A full services list helps you match the specific searches people make in a busy market.
  4. Post weekly. A short weekly update keeps your profile fresh among dozens of nearby competitors.
  5. Reply to every review. Thoughtful replies show future customers you care, and keep reviewers coming back.
  6. Plan for the growth corridors. If you serve the west, north or south-east, build pages for those regions rather than relying on the city alone.

Can you help my cafe or restaurant show up on Google Maps?

Yes. For hospitality, the Google Business Profile does most of the work: accurate hours, a full menu, regular photos, posts and a steady flow of reviews.

I serve all of Melbourne. Can I rank everywhere?

Your profile will rank most strongly near your location. Area pages and citations help you appear in more suburbs, and Google Ads can cover areas where organic rankings are harder.

Do you work with businesses in Geelong or regional Victoria?

Yes. We work with businesses anywhere in Australia, and set up area pages and tracking for the towns you serve.

How much does SEO cost for a Melbourne business?

It depends on your category and how competitive your suburbs are. You'll get a clear monthly price after the free audit, with no lock-in contract.

Can you redesign my website without losing rankings?

Yes. We record what ranks before we start, keep or redirect those pages and monitor rankings after launch.

How do I get more Google reviews?

Ask every happy customer, every time, with a direct link to your review form. We set up a simple process, including a review request email.

Do you work with businesses in Melbourne's outer suburbs?

Yes. We work with businesses across Greater Melbourne, from Werribee and Melton to Frankston and the Dandenongs, with area pages for the regions you serve.

Is it worth doing SEO for a single cafe or shop?

Yes. For a single location, a well-optimised Google Business Profile with regular photos and reviews is often the biggest source of new customers.

Can you help me compete with chains and franchises?

Yes. Local businesses often beat chains in the map pack with a more complete profile, better photos and more personal reviews.
