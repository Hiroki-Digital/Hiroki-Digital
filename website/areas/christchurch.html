Much of Canterbury's recent growth has been in the towns around Christchurch, including Rolleston, Lincoln and Rangiora. Customers there search locally first, and businesses that serve them need pages and reviews that connect them to those towns, not just to the city.

How we approach SEO for Christchurch businesses

We start with a heatmap across your service area, including the towns you cover, and compare the top 10 for your keyword. We then fix your Google Business Profile: categories, services, descriptions, profile features, FAQs and weekly photos.

On the website, we add your service and Christchurch to title tags and headings, your details to the footer, schema and a map, and pages for each area. For trades, NZ platforms such as NoCowboys and Builderscrack are often worth adding alongside Yellow and Finda.

Construction and trade businesses usually start with local SEO across the city and towns and NZ trade directories. If your website is older, a redesign often comes first.

Local SEO checklist for Christchurch businesses

Whether you work with us or not, these are the steps that make the biggest difference to local rankings in Christchurch.

  1. Include the satellite towns. If you work in Rolleston, Lincoln, Rangiora or Kaiapoi, say so and build pages for them.
  2. Show your project work. For builders and trades, photos and short write-ups of local projects build trust quickly.
  3. Use NZ trade directories. NoCowboys and Builderscrack sit alongside Yellow and Finda for trade businesses.
  4. Complete your profile. Categories, services, hours, bookings link and FAQs all count.
  5. Keep your NAP consistent. Make sure any old addresses from before a move are updated everywhere.
  6. Collect reviews from across Canterbury. Reviews from customers in each town help you rank there.
