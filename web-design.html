Website Design FAQs

Ask anything else in the form and we'll cover it on our call.

How much does a website cost?

You get a fixed quote after the discovery call, based on the number of pages and features such as online booking, payments or a blog. The price we quote is the price you pay.

How long does it take?

Most small-business websites launch in 3 to 6 weeks. Larger sites with online stores or custom features take longer, and your quote includes a timeline.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We keep what's working, such as pages that already rank on Google, and set up redirects so you don't lose search traffic when the new site goes live.

Will I be able to update it myself?

Yes. You'll be able to change text, swap images and publish posts from a simple editor. We include a recorded walkthrough so you can refer back to it.

Which platform do you build on?

It depends on how you'll use the site. We usually recommend WordPress or Webflow for sites you'll edit often, and Shopify for online stores. We'll explain the trade-offs before you decide.

Do you write the content?

We can. Usually you give us the facts about your business and we turn them into clear, search-friendly page copy. You review and approve everything before launch.
