Search in a spread-out, fast-growing city

Greater Brisbane covers a wide area, from Redcliffe in the north to Logan and Springfield in the south and Ipswich to the west. Customers search with suburb names, and Google's map results change quickly as you move across the city.

How we approach SEO for Brisbane businesses

We begin with your main keyword and a heatmap across your service area, then compare the top-ranking businesses on reviews, photos, categories and services. In growth areas, the gap is often small, and a fully completed profile can move you into the top three quickly.

Next comes the website: title tags naming your service and Brisbane, a clear H1, your details in the footer, schema and an embedded map. Where you serve several regions, we build pages for each one and link them to the relevant services.

For Brisbane businesses, we often recommend combining local SEO with focused landing pages for their ads. If your site is slow or dated, a website redesign usually comes first.

Local SEO checklist for Brisbane businesses

Whether you work with us or not, these are the steps that make the biggest difference to local rankings in Brisbane.

  1. Claim your growth suburbs early. In newer areas like North Lakes or Springfield, an early, complete profile can lock in rankings before competitors arrive.
  2. Separate north side and south side. If you serve both, build a page for each so Google and customers see you cover them.
  3. Use Queensland time. Make sure your hours on Google reflect Queensland time, which doesn't change in summer.
  4. Photograph your work locally. Weekly photos of real jobs around Brisbane help your profile stay active and trustworthy.
  5. Build citations on Australian directories. Consistent listings on platforms like Yellow Pages and TrueLocal support your map rankings.
  6. Keep reviews coming. A review request after every job keeps your profile ahead as more businesses move into Brisbane.
