How we approach SEO for Auckland businesses

We follow the same order every time: Google Business Profile first, then the website, then citations. On the profile, that means one sharp primary category, a full services list with descriptions, a bookings link, FAQs and weekly photos and posts.

For citations, New Zealand has its own platforms. Depending on your industry, we look at Yellow, Finda, NoCowboys and Builderscrack, plus Bing Places and Apple Business Connect, and the directories ranking for your keyword in Auckland.

Auckland service businesses usually start with local SEO area by area. Trades get extra value from NZ directory citations, and Google Ads can cover areas where organic rankings take longer.

Local SEO checklist for Auckland businesses

Whether you work with us or not, these are the steps that make the biggest difference to local rankings in Auckland.

  1. Choose your areas carefully. Decide which parts of Auckland you really serve, and build pages for those first.
  2. Use local area names. Aucklanders search with names like the North Shore, West Auckland and suburb names. Use them naturally.
  3. Get on NZ directories. Yellow and Finda suit most businesses, and trades benefit from NoCowboys and Builderscrack.
  4. Add a full services list. Describe every service on your profile so you match specific searches.
  5. Post and add photos weekly. Fresh activity keeps your profile competitive in a large market.
  6. Keep reviews steady. Ask every happy customer, every time, with a direct link to your review form.
