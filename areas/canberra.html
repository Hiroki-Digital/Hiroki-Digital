How we approach SEO for Canberra businesses

Every Canberra project starts with a heatmap across the city and a look at the top 10 businesses for your keyword. We compare reviews, photos, categories and services, and check for fake listings, which are easier to spot in a smaller market.

Then we work through your Google Business Profile, your website and citations. Area pages for each district you serve, linked to your service pages, help you appear beyond your immediate centre, and Queanbeyan can be included even though it's across the border in New South Wales.

For Canberra businesses, we usually recommend starting with local SEO across the districts and content for each area they serve. Email follow-up then helps to turn more enquiries into bookings.

Local SEO checklist for Canberra businesses

Whether you work with us or not, these are the steps that make the biggest difference to local rankings in Canberra.

  1. Target the districts you serve. Build pages for Belconnen, Gungahlin, Woden, Tuggeranong and the inner north and south as needed.
  2. Include Queanbeyan if you cover it. Many Canberra customers live across the border, so say clearly that you serve Queanbeyan.
  3. Show your credentials. For government and professional buyers, list qualifications, accreditations and capability clearly.
  4. Keep your profile complete. Hours, services, bookings link and FAQs all help in a city where people compare carefully.
  5. Add photos of local work. Photos from around Canberra show customers and Google that you're active in the area.
  6. Report clearly fake listings. In a smaller market, fake listings are easier to spot. We report them with evidence when we're sure.
